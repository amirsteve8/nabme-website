NABME Organizational Impact Report · 2016–2026

From grassroots PD to a national movement.

Open the full report

Founded in 2016 and self-funded for five years by its founder, Dr. Rictor Craig, NABME reached over 10,000 educators through free instructional professional development across D.C.

In 2021, NewSchools Venture Fund became NABME's first major investor, catalyzing the Athari Fellowship, the Capital Summit, and a national movement spanning 20+ states.

Athari Fellowship Impact Report · 2021–2026

100% retained. 83% in leadership.

Open the full report

Across five years and four cohorts, the Athari Fellowship has served 31 Black male educators. All 18 Fellows in Cohorts 1 and 2 are still in education, and 15 of 18 hold formal leadership positions.

Black Male Educator Capital Summit Impact Report · 2026

310+ educators demanded entry.

Open the full report

On February 28, 2026, the inaugural Summit brought together 190+ registered Black male educators, leaders and allies in Washington, D.C. A waitlist of 120+ more signaled demand that exceeded capacity by more than 60%.
