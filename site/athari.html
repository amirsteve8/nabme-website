The Athari Fellowship

Athari (Swahili): impact.

A brotherhood, a leadership lab and a healing space for Black male educators across the DMV.

What Athari is

Two years. One brotherhood.

The Athari Fellowship is NABME's flagship leadership development program for Black male educators across the DMV region. It is a brotherhood, a leadership lab, and a healing space, built on four pillars that anchor every session, retreat, and convening.

Athari is a two-year program. Fellows meet monthly with seasoned school leaders, travel together, retreat together, and practice new leadership moves in their own buildings, with feedback from the men beside them and the supervisors above them.

The Fellowship is co-facilitated by Shawn Hardnett and Dr. Rictor Craig.

The Athari model

Four pillars anchor every session, retreat and convening.

I

Partnership

Accountable, vulnerable relationships among brothers who share lived experience and a common purpose.

II

Protection

The psychological safety to lower the guard, name what hurts and ask hard questions without performance.

III

Proximity

Physical and relational nearness to leaders who have walked the road, collapsing the distance between aspiration and example.

IV

Practice

Disciplined application of new frameworks to the daily work of leading schools, with feedback and reps.

Pillar IV · Practice

Fellows grew 80% to 124% in every leadership competency.

Athari measures leadership growth from three angles: Fellows' own self-assessment, 360 reviews completed by their direct supervisors, and before/after capability ratings. Across all three views, the evidence points the same direction.

BeforeAfter

Self-rating before vs. after the Fellowship · 1 = basic awareness, 4 = advanced (could teach it) · six Cohort 2 Fellows, end-of-year survey

4.10/5Mid-year supervisor 360 across 24 dimensions · 83% Agree or Strongly Agree

Inside an Athari year

Leadership is built in the room, on the road and at the table.

Summer

Summer Retreat

Shared housing to launch the cohort and build trust from day one.

Monthly

Leadership Classes

Co-facilitated by seasoned school leaders Shawn Hardnett and Dr. Rictor Craig.

On campus

Shadow Days

A day alongside sitting leaders at Statesmen Academy for Boys.

Winter

The Capital Summit

The field-wide convening of Black male educators that anchors the year.

On the road

BMEC-Philly

A travel cohort to the national Black Male Educators Convening.

Spring

The Lake House Retreat

Fireside dialogue and a cooking challenge, away from city, screens and school.
