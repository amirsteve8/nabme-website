Voices · March 7, 2023

Mathematics with Mr. BuggBey

A NABME Fellow on the joy of helping students gain confidence in math.

Confidence through learning.

Jeff BuggBey teaches fifth-grade math at Statesmen College Prep Academy for Boys. In NABME's profile, he describes the satisfaction of watching students understand a problem and gain confidence.

A network to grow with.

He credits NABME with connecting him to other Black male educators, practical training, conversations about shared challenges, and opportunities to present at conferences. He hopes to develop as a master teacher and take on leadership.
