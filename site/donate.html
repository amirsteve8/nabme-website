Donate

NABME needs your help.

When you invest in NABME, you are investing in changing the lives of millions of marginalized children across the world. NABME aspires to dramatically increase the number of highly effective Black and Brown male educators who positively impact the children that they serve. Your gift will help us get this done.

Give securely through PayPal

Every gift puts a Black male educator in the room.

Choose your amount, give once or monthly, and pay with a card or your PayPal account, all on NABME's secure PayPal page.

  • Pick any amount, from $25 to whatever moves you
  • Check "Make this a monthly donation" to give every month
  • Giving in honor of a Black male educator who shaped you? Add his name in the note
Go to PayPal to give

NABME is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. PayPal emails you a receipt for your records.

Proof it works

100% of our Athari Fellows are still in education, and 83% now lead.

More than 310 educators answered the call to the first Capital Summit. Read our impact reports.

With your support we can:

  • Establish connections with undergraduate institutions across the country to recruit Black males into the field of education
  • Provide high quality professional development to teachers to increase the quality of instruction in classrooms
  • Increase the interest of Black males in the field of education
  • Provide support to Black male educators in alignment with their professional goals
  • Bring Black male educators together at the annual Black Male Educator Capital Summit
